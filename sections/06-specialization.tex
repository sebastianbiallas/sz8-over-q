\section{A number field with group \texorpdfstring{$\Sz(8)$}{Sz(8)}}\label{sec:specialization}

We complete \cref{thm:main} with a rational fiber of the regular family.
Specialize $s=0$ in \eqref{eq:T2}, so $t=-7/5$, and put
$h(X)=f(X,-7/5)$. This is a monic degree-$65$ polynomial in
$\Z[1/65][X]$. Its reductions at $11$ and $31$ are separable and have
the following factorizations by degree:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ccc@{}}
\toprule
Prime&Factorization type&Frobenius order\\
\midrule
$11$&$1^2 7^9$&$7$\\
$31$&$13^5$&$13$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Separability at either prime proves separability over $\Q$. The map $T$
has no pole at $s=0$, and $h$ is separable, so this is an unramified
specialization of the generic splitting field. Its Galois group
$H$ embeds in $N$. The two reductions give elements of orders $7$ and
$13$ in $H$, so $91$ divides $|H|$.

The maximal subgroups of $N$ have orders
\[
 448,\qquad52,\qquad20,\qquad14.
\]
None is divisible by $91$. Hence $H=N$, proving the final assertion of
\cref{thm:main}.

The degree-$65$ field $\Q[X]/(h)$ has signature $(1,32)$.
Indeed, complex conjugation on the roots over the real base point $t=0$
has type $1\,2^{32}$, as certified by conjugate root enclosures.
There is no finite real branch point on the normalization, so this action
has the same cycle type along the real $t$-line. In particular, every
separable real specialization of $f$ has exactly one real root. The
verifier also checks the signature directly at $t=-7/5$.

For $s=0$, the monic integral polynomial $P$ in
\texttt{data/P\_number\_field.json} has degree $65$ and height $68$ digits.
It defines the same field as $h(X)=f(X,-7/5)$, whose primitive integer
height is $252$ digits. The file gives a rational polynomial $A$ taking
its root to a root of $h$; exact composition modulo the defining polynomial
and irreducibility verify the field isomorphism. An inverse is also
recorded.

Hilbert irreducibility also supplies infinitely many $\Sz(8)$ extensions
from the regular family. The specialization above gives a specific example
without requiring an ineffective choice of parameter.
