\begin{abstract}
We realize the Suzuki group $\Sz(8)$ regularly over $\Q$ by an explicit
degree-$65$ family over $\Q(s)$, and give a monic integral degree-$65$
polynomial over $\Q$ with this Galois group and $43$-digit coefficients.
Every three-point regular realization
of $\Aut(\Sz(8))=\Sz(8)\rtimes C_3$ over $\Q(t)$ has a rational cyclic
cubic quotient: it is ramified at exactly two geometric points, and its
odd degree makes its genus-zero curve rational over $\Q$.
We construct such a realization as a genus-six cover of degree $65$
branched at $\zeta_3,\zeta_3^2,\infty$, and pull back along an explicit
cubic map $t=T(s)$. A monic polynomial $f(X,t)$ of bidegree $(65,7)$
is certified directly from its coefficients by an exact discriminant
computation, a local argument removing $342$ apparent branch points,
and certified continuation.
\end{abstract}
